# Contact Override

The Contact section follows the global white-and-cyan palette and the established rounded component language.

## Container

- Use the shared expressive-section container: `max-w-[1800px]` with 24px phone gutters and 48px gutters from tablet widths upward.
- Apply those gutters inside the centered max-width container, matching Projects and Honors, rather than on the full-width section wrapper.
- Align the contact panel and footer columns with Skills, Projects, and Honors.
- Use a clean canvas without a grid background; subtle noise and cyan glow layers may remain.

## Shape

- Keep the main contact panel at the large card radius.
- Availability labels and CTA buttons use `rounded-full` pill geometry.
- The footer brand badge and social icon controls use circular `rounded-full` geometry.
- The return-to-top control uses a compact pill hover surface.
- Rounding must not change touch-target sizes, layout alignment, or hover stability.
